Rem Find
Rem Range.Find(what,After,LookIn,LookAt,Searchorder,SearchDirection,MatchCase,MatchByte,SearchFormat)

Rem what 搜索值
Rem After 起始位置，省略为range左上角
Rem Lookin xlValues、xlFormulas或者xlComments，默认值为xlFormulas
Rem LookAt xlWhole xlPart
Rem SearchOrder xlByROws 或 xlByColumns
Rem SearchDirection 向前查找（xlPrevious）还是向后查找（xlNext）
Rem MatchCase true，则搜索区分大小写。默认值为False
